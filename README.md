Two lines (274 characters) for the make.fun bounty
"Two Lines to Your Past Self": a warning to my past self about
one specific moment, and one sentence on why it mattered.

## The lines

> Don't sell the guitar for eighty dollars at the pawn shop on 5th Street the Tuesday after graduation.
>
> The eighty dollars was gone by Friday, but the guitar was the one thing that got you through the winter you lost your job, and you've spent ten years trying to buy it back.

274 characters, exactly two lines. The moment is concrete enough
to picture: a price, a place, a day of the week. The second line
traces real cause and effect instead of restating the warning: the
money vanished in three days, the guitar was the thing that carried
me through the winter I lost my job, and the loss is still being
paid for ten years later.

The field is crowded with 2 a.m. texts, Friday deploys, deleted
drafts and caches, voicemails, and breakup lines. A pawn-shop
sale of a guitar is a different lane: a small money decision
with a price tag on it, and a consequence measured in years.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```

Deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output. Standard
library only, no network, no subprocess, no eval.
